# Advice judge rubric

Score an advice answer from 1 to 5. Report the score on its own. Do not fold it
into the pass/fail rate.

- **5** — Specific to the user's actual tasks and dates, actionable, respects
  priorities and Shabbat, and invents no tasks.
- **4** — Specific and usable, with a small omission (a date, a priority, or
  Shabbat) that does not send the user the wrong way.
- **3** — Generic but correct.
- **2** — Mostly generic, or it leans on a fact the snapshot does not support.
- **1** — Wrong facts or invented tasks.

The answer must not claim to have changed anything. Planning, ordering, and
suggesting are not proposals. If the answer says a task was moved, completed,
deleted, or otherwise updated, score it 1.
